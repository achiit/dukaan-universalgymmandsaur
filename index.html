About Universal Gym

A Mandsaur fitness landmark since 2000.

LB
Lokesh BhargavOwner, Universal Gym

Universal Gym was established in 2000 and has been part of the Mandsaur fitness community ever since. Located in Kothari Nagar, it is a unisex fitness centre that welcomes both men and women.

The gym has a stated gymming area of more than 10,000 sq. ft. and offers separate luxurious training spaces, giving members room to train comfortably in a spacious environment.

With dedicated cardio and weight training facilities and clear membership options, Universal Gym is built around one idea: consistent training, every day.
